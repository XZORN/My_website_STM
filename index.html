Profile

First-year PhD student working at the boundary of growth and microscopy.

I am Zhuoran Xu, a first-year PhD student in the School of Physics at Peking University. I am currently doing research in Prof. Jian Wang's group at the International Center for Quantum Materials (ICQM).

My main experimental direction is MBE-STM: combining molecular beam epitaxy with scanning tunneling microscopy and spectroscopy to prepare, inspect, and understand quantum materials from the surface up.

MBE-STM

From growth chamber to tunneling junction.

The appeal of MBE-STM is continuity: a material can be grown, transferred, cooled, and measured without losing the fragile surface conditions that make atomic-scale physics visible.
