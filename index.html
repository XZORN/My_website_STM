Profile

Atomic-scale views of 2D superconductivity.

I am Zhuoran Xu, a first-year PhD student in the School of Physics at Peking University. I am currently doing research in Prof. Jian Wang's group at the International Center for Quantum Materials (ICQM).

My main experimental direction is MBE-STM: combining molecular beam epitaxy with scanning tunneling microscopy and spectroscopy to prepare, inspect, and understand MBE-grown quantum materials. I am particularly interested in local electronic structure, superconducting gap features, interface superconductivity, and the role of defects and impurities in two-dimensional systems.
