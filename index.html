Research

Atomic-scale imaging for strongly interacting electrons.

My work sits at the interface of surface preparation, cryogenic instrumentation, and spectroscopic mapping. I am interested in how local disorder, symmetry breaking, and moire-scale structure shape the electronic wavefunctions of low-dimensional materials.

Current directions include quasiparticle interference analysis, impurity-bound states, superconducting gap inhomogeneity, and STM-compatible device/sample architectures.
